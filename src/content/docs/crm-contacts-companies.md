In Workfyr OS, your B2B and B2C relationships are managed through **Contacts** and **Companies**.

## Companies (Accounts)
Companies represent the businesses you are selling to or partnering with.
- You can store their industry, website, and physical address.
- Multiple Contacts can be linked to a single Company.

## Contacts
Contacts represent the individual people you interact with. 
- You can link a Contact to a Company.
- If you are doing B2B sales, your Deals are typically attached to the Company, while the Contact acts as the champion or decision-maker.
- If you are doing B2C sales, Deals can be attached directly to the Contact.

## Communication
From the Contact detail view, you can log notes, schedule meetings, and send emails directly from the CRM.
