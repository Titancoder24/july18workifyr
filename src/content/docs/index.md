Workfyr OS is a comprehensive operating system for your business, bridging the gap between Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and Human Resource Management (HRM).

This documentation provides an end-to-end guide on how to utilize the various modules within the system.

## Navigation
- **App Shell & Sidebar**: Once logged in, the primary navigation is on your left. You can expand/collapse the sidebar or change its theme (Plain or Brand).
- **Omni-Search**: Use `Ctrl+K` (or `Cmd+K` on Mac) to bring up the global search palette to jump to deals, employees, or settings.

## Role Based Access Control
Workfyr OS utilizes Row Level Security (RLS). You only see what you are authorized to see.
- **Managers** have elevated privileges within their departments.
- **Sales Reps** can only see CRM data.
- **HR Admins** have full access to Payroll and Employee configuration.
