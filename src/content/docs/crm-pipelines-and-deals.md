The Workfyr CRM allows you to track revenue across custom sales pipelines.

## Pipelines
Pipelines represent different sales flows in your organization. You might have one pipeline for "Enterprise Sales" and another for "SMB Subscriptions".

### Managing Stages
Each pipeline consists of Stages.
1. Navigate to **CRM > Pipelines**.
2. Click **Add Stage** to define steps like *Lead In*, *Contact Made*, *Proposal Sent*, and *Closed Won*.
3. You can drag and drop stages to reorder them in the Kanban view.

## Deals
A Deal is a specific revenue opportunity attached to a Contact or Company.

### Creating a Deal
1. Navigate to the Kanban board.
2. Click **+ New Deal** in the desired stage.
3. Assign a monetary value, a close date, and an owner.

### Moving Deals
In the Kanban view, simply drag a deal card from one column to the next to update its status. When a deal reaches the *Closed Won* stage, it will automatically register as realized revenue in your Dashboard charts.
