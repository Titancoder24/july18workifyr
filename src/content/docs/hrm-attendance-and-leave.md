Workfyr OS includes a comprehensive system for tracking employee attendance and managing time off.

## Attendance Tracking
Employees can clock in and out using the Dashboard. 
- **Present**: Clocked in on time.
- **Late**: Clocked in after the designated start time.
- **Absent**: Did not clock in.

HR Admins can view the Daily Attendance Report under **HRM > Attendance** to see a bird's eye view of who is currently working.

## Leave Requests
Employees can request time off for vacations, sick days, or personal reasons.

1. Employee navigates to **HRM > Leave**.
2. They select a date range and a reason.
3. The request enters a `Pending` state.
4. An HR Admin or the Department Head will review the request and either `Approve` or `Reject` it.
5. Approved leave automatically syncs with Payroll calculations for that pay period.
