The Payroll system in Workfyr OS automates salary calculations based on employee attendance and base salary data.

## Running Payroll
Payroll is typically processed at the end of every month.

1. Navigate to **HRM > Payroll**.
2. Click **Generate Payroll**.
3. Select the target month (e.g., May 2026).
4. The system will automatically calculate the net salary for each employee by reading their Base Salary and subtracting unpaid leave or absences.

## Payslips
Once a payroll run is completed and approved, employees will automatically be able to view and download their Payslips from their personal Dashboard.

### Deductions and Bonuses
HR Admins can manually add ad-hoc deductions or bonuses to specific employees before a payroll run is finalized. These will appear as separate line items on the generated payslip.
