The Employee Directory is the core of the HRM module. It acts as the central source of truth for your entire workforce.

## Managing Employees
When you onboard a new employee, you will assign them to a Department and grant them specific Roles.

### Provisioning
1. Navigate to **HRM > Directory**.
2. Click **+ Add Employee**.
3. Input their legal name, work email, and job title.
4. Assign them a base salary. This value is strictly isolated and can only be seen by HR Admins and the employee themselves due to Supabase Row Level Security.

## Departments
You can organize employees into Departments (e.g., Engineering, Sales, Marketing). Department heads can be assigned to automatically approve leave requests for employees within their department.
