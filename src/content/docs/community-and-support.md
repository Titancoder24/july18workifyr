Workfyr OS integrates a public community forum and a private support ticketing system.

## Community Forum
The Community Forum allows users to discuss features, ask questions, and share workflows.
- Users can post new threads.
- Users can comment on existing threads.
- Super Admins can moderate the forum from the Admin Panel by deleting inappropriate posts or comments.

## Support Tickets
If a user encounters a bug or needs assistance, they can submit a Support Ticket via the `/help` route.
- Tickets are sent securely to the `support_tickets` database table.
- Super Admins can view and respond to tickets from the Admin Panel.
- The Admin Dashboard tracks open tickets to ensure SLAs are met.
