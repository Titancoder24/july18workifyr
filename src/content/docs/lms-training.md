The Learning Management System (LMS) allows you to host educational content and training videos for your employees or users.

## Uploading Videos
The Admin Panel controls the LMS content.
1. Log into the **Admin Panel**.
2. Navigate to **Content Management > LMS Videos**.
3. Add a new video module by providing a title, description, and the video embed URL (e.g., from YouTube or Vimeo).

## Consuming Content
Users can access the LMS through the public `/learn` route. The videos are displayed in a modern grid format, allowing users to watch tutorials, training materials, and company-wide broadcasts.
