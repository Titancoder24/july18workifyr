Workfyr OS makes it easy to generate invoices and track payments.

## Creating an Invoice
1. Navigate to **CRM > Invoices**.
2. Click **Create Invoice**.
3. Select an existing Company or Contact.
4. Add line items. The system will automatically calculate sub-totals, tax, and the final grand total.

## Invoice Statuses
- **Draft**: The invoice is being prepared and has not been sent.
- **Sent**: The invoice has been emailed to the client.
- **Paid**: The client has completed the payment. Paid invoices count towards your Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) and Total Revenue metrics.
- **Overdue**: The due date has passed without payment.

## Exporting
You can export any invoice to PDF directly from the Invoice Details view by clicking the **Download PDF** button.
