Managing leads is the first step in the sales pipeline for Workfyr OS. Leads represent potential customers who have shown interest but have not yet been qualified into active Deals.

## Adding a Lead
1. Navigate to **CRM > Leads**.
2. Click **+ New Lead**.
3. Fill out the lead's contact information and the *Source* (e.g., Organic Search, Referral, Cold Call).

## Qualifying Leads
Leads typically move through statuses:
- **New**: Recently captured, untouched.
- **Contacted**: A sales representative has reached out.
- **Qualified**: The lead fits your customer profile and is ready to be converted.
- **Unqualified**: The lead is not a good fit for your business.

When a Lead is *Qualified*, you should convert them into a **Contact** or **Company**, and then attach a **Deal** to them.
