Departments allow you to logically group employees into distinct business units within Workfyr OS.

## Creating a Department
1. Navigate to **HRM > Directory** and switch to the **Departments** tab.
2. Click **+ Add Department**.
3. Provide a name and assign a *Department Head*.

## Department Head Privileges
The Department Head is automatically granted elevated privileges over their subordinates. 
- They can view attendance records for their department.
- They are responsible for approving or rejecting **Leave Requests** originating from employees within their business unit.
